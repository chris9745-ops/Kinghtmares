League rules

From the league's flag football rules sheet for grades K to 12. The original sheet is at the bottom of this page.

The short version

  • No contact
  • The quarterback can't run
  • 3 downs to reach midfield, 3 more to score
  • 10 seconds for the quarterback to pass
  • One blitz per series, from 7 or more yards back
  • No flag guarding or blocking

Game setup

  • 5 players maximum on the field, 3 minimum.
  • Two 25-minute halves with a 5-minute halftime.
  • More than 10 minutes late is a forfeit.
  • Each team gets one 60-second timeout.
  • A coin toss determines first possession.

Offense

  • No contact, blocking, or pick plays.
  • The offense starts at its 5-yard line.
  • 3 plays to cross midfield. After crossing, 3 more plays to score.
  • No punts or field goals.
  • The ball must be snapped by a center to the quarterback.
  • No direct snaps to running backs or receivers.
  • The quarterback cannot run, even if blitzed.
  • Handoffs and pitches are allowed.
  • Grades 3 and up have no-running zones within 5 yards of the end zone. The offense must pass.

Running

  • Any player but the quarterback may run.
  • Handoffs and pitches are allowed. No forward pass after a handoff past the line of scrimmage.
  • Grades 3 and up have no-running zones within 5 yards of the end zone. The offense must pass.
  • Spinning is allowed, but a runner cannot leave their feet to avoid a defender.

Passing and receiving

  • The quarterback has 10 seconds to pass. If not, the play is dead, it is a loss of down, and the ball returns to the original line of scrimmage.
  • Once the quarterback hands off, the 10-second rule is no longer in effect.
  • All players are eligible receivers, including the quarterback after a handoff behind the line.
  • Only one player in motion at a time.
  • A receiver must have at least one foot in bounds for a catch.

Defense

  • Non-blitzing defenders cannot cross the line of scrimmage until the ball is handed off or lateraled.
  • One blitz is permitted per series.
  • Blitzers must be at least 7 yards behind the line of scrimmage when the ball is snapped.

When a ball carrier is down

The play ends when:

  • Their flag is pulled.
  • Their knee hits the ground.
  • They go out of bounds.
  • Their flag falls off and they have first contact with a defender.

The ball is spotted where the player's belt was, not where the ball is.

Scoring and turnovers

Scoring

  • Touchdown: 6 points.
  • Safety: 2 points.
  • No extra-point attempts.

Turnovers

  • Interceptions can be returned.
  • A fumble that hits the ground is a dead play. The offense keeps possession and cannot advance the fumble.
  • A fumble into the offense's own end zone is a safety.

Penalties

Delay of game

  • 30 seconds to snap.
  • First offense is a warning, then 5 yards.

Offsides or illegal motion

  • 5 yards.

Defensive penalties

  • Pass interference, illegal contact, illegal flag pulling, illegal rushing and similar: 5 yards and an automatic first down.

Offensive penalties

  • Illegal forward pass, pass interference, flag guarding, illegal picks, illegal blocking and similar: 5 yards and loss of down.

Intentional tackling, elbowing, cheap shots

  • Warning, then a penalty, then an automatic first down or loss of down, then ejection for repeats.

Trash talking

  • Prohibited. Repeated offenses can lead to ejection.

Equipment

  • Cleats are allowed. No metal spikes.
  • Shirts must be tucked in, and clothing cannot interfere with access to flags.
  • Grades 5 and 6 play with a size 7 football. If that size isn't available, any ball both coaches agree on can be used.

Coaches' responsibilities

  • Spot the ball after each play, keep track of the line of scrimmage, place the 7-yard marker for the defense, and call all penalties.
  • Be fair and courteous. No arguing or inappropriate language.
  • Ensure all team members are fairly treated.
